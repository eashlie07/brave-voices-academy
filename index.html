ACT II ABOUT

Theater shouldn't be something you have to leave town for.

For families in Teller County, finding year-round theater education can mean a long drive, a summer-only program, or waiting until high school.

Brave Voices Academy exists to change that.

We create a place right here in our community where young performers can play, experiment, make friends, take creative risks, and find their voice.

Our classes are designed for kids who already love the stage, but also for kids who aren't quite sure if the stage is for them yet.

You don't have to be the loudest kid in the room to belong onstage.

THE BRAVE VOICES IDEA

"Every kid deserves a place to explore, play, and feel accepted through theater, even if they never want to be a performer."

— Elio Ashlie, Founder of Brave Voices Academy

ACT III PROGRAMS

What are you ready to try?

Theater looks different for every performer. That's why Brave Voices offers different ways to step into the room.

01

EXPLORE

Start somewhere playful.

01

Brave Voices Improv

Exploring Creativity Through Play!

Improv is all about imagination, teamwork, and having fun. Kids play games, create scenes, and build stories together—with no scripts and no pressure.

  • Build confidence speaking in front of others
  • Practice listening and teamwork
  • Strengthen creativity and problem-solving
  • Learn to think on their feet
AGES 7–10 CLASS SIZE 4–15
02

Brave Voices Musical Theater

A First Step Into the Spotlight!

An introduction to the language and joy of musical theater. Young performers learn choreography, music, stage terminology, ensemble work, and get their own moment to shine.

  • Learn stage directions and theater lingo
  • Build coordination through choreography
  • Practice singing and moving together
  • Discover the joy of being part of a cast
AGES 7–10 CLASS SIZE 4–15
02

BUILD

Take the next step.

03

Brave Voices Acting Intensive

Bring Scripts to Life!

A two-part acting intensive focused on scripted scene work and monologue performance. Students learn how to listen, react, memorize, and tell a story with intention.

  • Build confidence performing in front of others
  • Learn memorization strategies
  • Understand the rules of the stage
  • Discover their unique acting voice
AGES 11–14 CLASS SIZE 4–15
04

Brave Voices Musical Theater

Acting Through Song!

Singing is one thing. Telling a story while you sing is another. Students choose an audition-length song and learn to perform it with intention, emotion, and character.

  • Connect emotion to voice and movement
  • Receive personalized performance feedback
  • Practice performing with intention
  • Leave with a polished audition piece
AGES 11–14 CLASS SIZE 4–15
03

RETURN

Come back to the family.

05

RETURNING PERFORMERS ONLY

Brave Voices Throwback Camp

The Best of Brave Voices, Revisited!

A celebration for our veteran Brave Voices performers. Returning students revisit favorite games, scenes, songs, and moments while taking the lead and sharing their own ideas with friends.

  • Strengthen leadership and collaboration
  • Revisit favorite theater activities
  • Celebrate growth as a performer
  • Reconnect with the Brave Voices community
AGES 7–14 CLASS SIZE 4–15
